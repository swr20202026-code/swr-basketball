The scrimmage

Where it all started.

Just turn up, hoop and compete. Our open scrimmage brings players together for competitive basketball in a welcoming environment. Book your spot online, lace up and come ready to play.

  • 1Pick a run and book your place online.
  • 2Pay securely when you book and your spot is confirmed.
  • 3We need a minimum of 10 players. If a run doesn't go ahead, everyone who booked receives a refund.
The club

From the runs to the competitive game.

South West Rise is the competitive side of our basketball community. From developing the next generation through our junior programme to competing with our men's team, we're building a club where players can develop, compete and rise together.
